Production Flow

The Wedding Video Process

From initial date reservation to final master handover, we maintain clear milestones and preserve your family moments with respect.

01

Ritual Consultation

We review your ceremony schedule, auspicious muhurat timings, key family members, song selections, and specific filming requirements.

02

Shooting or Ingest

On-site shooting in Katni with multi-camera coverage, or direct ingestion of raw wedding camera cards from studios and couples across India.

03

Story Pacing & Grading

We carefully craft the narrative arc, balance festive colors, sweeten audio, and compose custom ceremony titling and transitions.

04

Family Master Handover

Receive high-definition master files organized on USB storage drives for television viewing, alongside compressed mobile files for easy sharing.
