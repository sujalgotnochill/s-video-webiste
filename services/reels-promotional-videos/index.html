Production Flow

The Reel Creation Process

A fast, structured turnaround process designed to deliver mobile-ready video content without unnecessary delays.

01

Objective & Hook

We discuss the target audience, core message, key call-to-action, and the opening visual style to engage viewers.

02

9:16 Selection & Framing

We crop or import high-quality vertical footage, ensuring key subjects remain in the safe viewing area away from platform UI overlays.

03

Motion, Text & Audio

We add kinetic typography, subtle zooms, sound effects, and rhythm-synchronized transitions to maintain viewer interest throughout.

04

Mobile Export

The finished reel is exported in optimal H.264 vertical bitrates for Instagram and YouTube, ensuring crisp playback without compression artifacts.
