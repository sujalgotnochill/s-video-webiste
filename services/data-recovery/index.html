Storage Media & Data Recovery Services

Accidental deletions, card read errors, or formatted media can put irreplaceable ceremony footage and creative projects at risk. S. Video Creation offers dedicated diagnostic evaluation and extraction services for camera SD cards, external hard drives, and flash drives in Katni.

Important Technical Notice Regarding Data Recovery

Data recovery feasibility depends directly on the physical integrity of the storage media and whether deleted sectors have been overwritten by new files. Please stop writing new files or taking new photos on the affected card or drive immediately. Continuing to use the card or running unverified consumer utilities may cause permanent data loss. We perform an initial diagnostic intake to assess recovery viability; however, recovery cannot be guaranteed for physically degraded, damaged, or overwritten storage media.

Diagnostic Scope

Common Recovery Scenarios & Media Types

We evaluate logical read issues, accidental format incidents, and missing directory structures across standard consumer and camera media.

Camera SD & MicroSD Cards

Evaluation of SD cards from mirrorless cameras, DSLRs, and handheld recorders showing "Card Cannot Be Read" or "Need to Format" warnings after shoots.

Media: SDHC, SDXC & MicroSD

External USB Hard Drives

Assessment of portable USB hard drives containing project backups where file partitions have become unallocated, RAW, or accidentally cleared.

Media: Portable USB 3.0/Type-C HDDs

USB Flash & Pen Drives

Scanning flash drives experiencing corrupted file allocation tables (FAT/exFAT), unreadable directories, or abrupt disconnection errors during transfers.

Media: Standard USB Flash Drives

Accidental File Deletion

Deep carving for accidentally deleted video clips (MP4, MOV, MTS) and raw camera files before memory sectors are overwritten with new data.

Scenario: Empty Trash / Quick Delete

Quick-Formatted Cards

When a camera card is accidentally quick-formatted in camera or on a computer, we inspect the unallocated sectors to reconstruct original file blocks.

Scenario: In-Camera Quick Format

Local clients can hand-deliver their media directly to our Katni studio. Physical inspection avoids risky courier transit for already unstable cards.

Technical Protocol

The Diagnostic & Recovery Procedure

We follow safe recovery protocols designed to protect source media from additional stress or data corruption.

01

Media Isolation

The storage device is mounted with write-protection enabled so no operating system processes or temporary files can write to the affected card.

02

Diagnostic Scan

We perform a low-level sector analysis to detect remaining file headers, partition boundaries, and evaluate the volume of recoverable data.

03

Safe Extraction

Identified video and image files are extracted strictly to a separate, healthy destination drive—never back onto the compromised source media.

04

File Verification

Recovered video containers are inspected for playback integrity. The client confirms satisfaction before the recovered data is handed over.
