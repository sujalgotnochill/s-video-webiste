Standard Procedure

How We Handle Every Project

A structured workflow ensures creative fidelity, technical accuracy, and safe media handling for projects both large and small.

01

Consultation & Brief

We discuss your project goals, desired duration, music preferences, delivery timeline, and output aspect ratios (16:9 widescreen or 9:16 vertical).

02

Media Intake & Backup

Bring your raw camera media or drive to our Katni studio, or share high-bitrate files via secure cloud storage for remote editing orders.

03

Crafting & Technical Work

Our studio handles timeline assembly, color grading, sound synchronization, transition mixing, or storage media diagnostic extraction.

04

Review & Master Handover

Review watermarked draft previews, request adjustments, and receive high-resolution final master exports ready for broadcast or sharing.
