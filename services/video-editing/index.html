Production Flow

The Video Editing Workflow

From raw clips to final master files, our editing workflow emphasizes organization, communication, and structured milestone reviews.

01

Ingest & Organize

Raw footage is ingested from camera cards or drives, backed up to working drives, and organized by scene, camera angle, or chronology.

02

Rough Assembly

We construct the initial timeline, trimming dead frames, placing key narrative points, and aligning primary audio tracks to set the overall pace.

03

Fine Cut & Color Polish

Cuts are tightened to rhythm, color grading is applied to unify tones, audio levels are balanced, and titles and transitions are refined.

04

Export & Handover

Clients review a watermarked draft for final feedback. Upon approval, high-resolution master video files are delivered via drive or secure cloud link.
