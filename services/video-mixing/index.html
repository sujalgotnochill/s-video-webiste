Production Flow

The Video Mixing Workflow

How we take multiple camera cards and separate audio lines and synthesize them into a polished, uninterrupted master video.

01

Sync & Angle Grid

All camera footage and external audio stems are synchronized using audio waveform matching and timecode alignments into a multi-camera grid.

02

Timeline Angle Selection

We cut across the timeline, selecting primary views, cutting to reaction shots, and tracking key stage movements with natural visual pacing.

03

Transitions & Titling

The project is enriched with event transitions, ceremony titling, family lower thirds, and synchronized musical enhancement.

04

Master Delivery

The final mix is exported into Full HD or 4K broadcast masters, packaged onto external USB storage drives or shared via secure cloud transfer.
